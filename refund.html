Refund Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

1. Order Cancellation

Because products are ordered using Cash on Delivery, contact us as soon as possible if you need to cancel an order. Cancellation is subject to whether the order has already been processed or dispatched.

2. Returns

For an eligible product return, contact support@kanhaandsons.com with the order details and the reason for the return. The product may need to be returned in its original condition and with included accessories.

3. Damaged or Incorrect Product

If an item arrives damaged or is materially different from the product ordered, contact us promptly with clear details. We may request photographs or other information needed to assess the issue.

4. Refunds

Where a refund is approved, the refund method and timing will depend on the circumstances and payment method. For Cash on Delivery orders, any approved refund will be handled directly rather than through an online payment gateway.

5. Sling TV Subscriptions

We do not sell or process Sling TV subscriptions. Any refund, cancellation or billing request relating to a Sling TV service must be directed to Sling TV's official support channels.
