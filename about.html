ABOUT

Independent information, clearly presented.

HARSH CHAUHAN is an independent online resource providing educational Sling TV information and a small shop of generic TV accessories.

Who We Are

This website is owned and operated by HARSH CHAUHAN. We publish practical guides about Sling TV plans, channels, supported devices, setup and common troubleshooting topics.

Our Mission

Streaming services can be confusing when plan names, device compatibility and troubleshooting steps are spread across different help pages. Our goal is to organize that information into straightforward guides that are easy to understand.

Our Shop

The shop offers generic TV and streaming accessories such as universal remotes, HDMI cables, network accessories and mounting products. These products are not represented as Sling TV-branded or officially supplied by Sling TV.

Transparency

We do not have access to customer Sling TV accounts and cannot change subscriptions, issue official Sling refunds or provide official account support. When an issue requires account-level action, we direct readers to Sling's official resources.
