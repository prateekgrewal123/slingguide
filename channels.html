CHANNEL GUIDE

Sling TV channel guide.

Sling TV channel availability depends on the base plan, extras, location and current programming agreements. Use the categories below to understand how to compare channels.

Sports

Sports-focused programming is a major part of Sling's Orange offering and optional sports packages.

News

News programming is prominent in Blue and can be expanded with news-focused extras.

Entertainment

Entertainment, lifestyle and reality programming varies by plan and add-on.

Family

Family-oriented channels are available across selected plans and extras.

Check the live lineup before subscribing.

Channel availability and package placement can change. The official Sling channel guide is the best source for the current lineup.

Why a channel may be unavailable

A channel can be missing because it belongs to a different base plan, requires an Extra, is subject to local availability, or has changed as programming agreements are updated. Compare the channel against the exact plan you have rather than relying on an old screenshot or list.

How to compare channels efficiently

Make a short list of the channels you actually watch. Then compare that list against Orange, Blue, Orange & Blue and the smaller Select/Essentials options. Finally, check whether an Extra is cheaper than moving to a larger package. This approach avoids paying for a large number of channels you may never use.
