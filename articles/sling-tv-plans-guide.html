Sling TV Plans Guide

Understand the major Sling TV plan choices and how to compare them without relying on outdated pricing pages.

Start with the base plan

Sling TV offers several base options, including Select, Orange, Blue and the combined Orange & Blue package. The right choice depends on the channels you actually watch, how many people need to stream at once and whether you need sports or news extras.

Select

Sling Select is positioned as a smaller, budget-focused package. It can make sense when your priority is a lower starting price and a limited set of core channels. Check the current channel list before subscribing because the lineup can change.

Orange

Orange is designed around sports and family programming. Current Sling information lists a single simultaneous stream for the base Orange service. If sports is the reason you are considering Sling, compare the current Orange channel list and any Sports Extra options.

Blue

Blue focuses more heavily on news and entertainment. Sling currently lists up to three streams for Blue. Compare the exact channels you need rather than assuming that a larger stream allowance automatically makes Blue the better option.

Orange & Blue

The combined package brings the Orange and Blue selections together and is intended for households that want broader sports, news and entertainment coverage. It costs more than either base package, so compare the channels you would otherwise need through Extras.

Extras and passes

Sling also offers add-ons such as Sports Extra and News Extra. Shorter-duration passes may be available for eligible services. Read the specific offer terms because passes and promotional pricing can have different conditions from normal monthly subscriptions.

How to choose

Write down your must-have channels first. Then compare those channels against the base plans and Extras. Finally, compare the total monthly cost, simultaneous streams and promotional renewal terms. This prevents choosing a package simply because it has the largest channel count.

Verify before subscribing

Prices, channel lineups, promotions, taxes, fees and eligibility can change. Use the official Sling plan comparison as the final check before purchasing a subscription.
