SLING TV ACCOUNT

Sling TV Account & Billing Guide

Understand common Sling TV account and billing questions and where official action is required.

Account access

Use the email associated with your Sling account. If you cannot sign in, use the official recovery process rather than repeatedly guessing credentials.

Plan changes

Base plans and Extras can change the channels and price of your service. Review the current offer and renewal details before making a change.

Renewals and promotions

Promotional pricing may apply for a limited period. Read the offer details to understand what happens after the promotion ends and what rate may apply afterward.

Billing questions

For charges, invoices, payment methods or subscription status, use Sling’s official account and support tools. We cannot access customer billing records.

Refund questions

We do not process refunds for Sling TV subscriptions. Subscription refund requests must be directed to Sling under its current policies and account procedures.

Keep your account secure

Use a strong unique password and avoid sharing account credentials. If you suspect unauthorized access, follow Sling’s official account-security and recovery guidance.
