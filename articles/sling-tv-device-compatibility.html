Sling TV Device Compatibility Guide

Learn how to check whether your television, streaming device, phone or browser is compatible with Sling TV.

Supported categories

Sling’s official device list includes smart TVs, streaming platforms, mobile devices, browsers and gaming platforms. The exact list can change, so check the official page for your model.

Smart TVs

Supported Samsung, LG and other compatible televisions can run the Sling app. The operating system version and exact model can matter.

Streaming devices

Platforms such as Roku, Amazon Fire TV and Android TV/Google TV are among the device categories supported by Sling. Always verify the current supported-device list rather than relying on a generic platform name.

Phones and computers

Sling provides mobile and browser options for supported devices. Browser compatibility and operating-system requirements can change as software is updated.

Why an older device may fail

An older device can stop being supported even if a newer model from the same product family works. If the app disappears from the app store or repeatedly crashes after updates, check Sling’s compatibility documentation.
