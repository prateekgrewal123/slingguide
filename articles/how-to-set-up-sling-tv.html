SLING TV SETUP

How to Set Up Sling TV on a TV

A practical setup sequence for installing Sling TV on a compatible television or streaming device.

Check compatibility first

Use Sling’s current supported-device list to confirm your TV or streaming platform. Compatibility can depend on the exact model and software version.

Connect the TV to the internet

Complete the television or streaming device network setup. A stable connection is important for live streaming.

Install the Sling app

Open the device’s app store, search for Sling TV and install the official application. Avoid downloading apps from unofficial sources.

Sign in

Open the Sling app and sign in using the credentials associated with your Sling subscription. If you do not have an account, use Sling’s official sign-up flow.

Test playback

Open a channel included with your plan and allow the stream to load. If playback is unstable, troubleshoot the network before changing multiple settings.

Finish your setup

Once the app works reliably, review picture, audio and device settings. Keep the app and device software updated for the best chance of continued compatibility.
