SLING TV PLANS

Sling TV plan guide.

Sling TV offers several base plans and add-on options. Plan pricing and offers can change, so use this page as an educational overview and verify the live offer before subscribing.

SELECT$19.99/mo.

A smaller budget-oriented base package. Current Sling information lists Select as a base option.

ORANGE$45.99/mo.

Sports and family-oriented programming. Current official information lists one stream for the base Orange service.

BLUE$45.99/mo.

News and entertainment-oriented programming. Current official information lists up to three streams for Blue.

ORANGE & BLUE$60.99/mo.

A combined option with a broader channel selection and more simultaneous-stream flexibility.

Important: verify current pricing

Prices above are a September 2026 snapshot. Promotions, taxes, channel lineups, stream limits and offer terms can change.

Extras and passes

Sling also offers add-ons such as Sports Extra and News Extra, along with shorter-duration options on eligible services. These can be useful when you want a particular category without moving to a larger base package.

How to choose a plan

Start with the channels and viewing habits that matter most. Sports-heavy households may prefer Orange, while news and entertainment viewers may prefer Blue. If you want the broader combination, compare Orange & Blue. For a smaller budget, review Select and other current offers. Always confirm the exact channel lineup and current offer terms before subscribing.

Before you subscribe

Check the total price, promotional duration, renewal terms, supported devices, simultaneous streams, available channels and any extra fees or taxes. If you are using a pass or prepay option, read the specific offer conditions because those terms can differ from a normal monthly subscription.
